# Versioned Will contract

The engine enforces the uploaded `will-contract/0.1` shape for six additive
record domains:

- `will_definition`: nature, classification, ancestry evidence, generative
  boundary, invariants, and inherent limits; it does not require a bearer.
- `will_expression`: exact definition and bearer, interpretation, channels,
  typed property bindings, learned techniques, and awakening access/mastery.
- `will_technique`: derivation, practice status, trigger, channels, typed
  effects, declared costs, resolution policy, and termination conditions.
- `will_awakening`: qualitative change, substrates, eligibility/activation,
  transformed techniques, upkeep, interruption, residue, and recovery.
- `will_development`: one evidenced claim in one of eight dimensions; it does
  not award a universal score or unlock an entire personality/domain.
- `will_effect`: active occurrence with source event, targets, anchors, clock,
  process/consequence links, ending conditions, and persistent residue.

Every contract carries `schema_version`, immutable `id`/`version`, a separate
`design_status` (`proposed` or `accepted`), and provenance evidence. Exact
record and rule references are checked in the current branch; ancestry cycles,
wrong domains, missing events, invalid scopes, incompatible units, negative
costs, and duplicate contribution keys are rejected.

Will attempts are separate execution records. Preparation evaluates feasibility
and mechanical conditions before reserving one stable random result. Commit is
atomic and uses only the stored, engine-compiled result. Unknown mechanics,
including the numerical freedom formula, restrained intelligence, and detailed
Haki rules, remain GM judgments or proposed rules.
