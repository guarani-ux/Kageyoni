# Constellation Substrate Protocol

This skill encodes the developmental capability system that enables situational co-presence, grounded alchemical transmutation, and frame-releasing regrounding across Kageyōni.

---

## 1. Core Primitives & Invariant Trichotomy

- **Constellation**: The situated addressable field (people, tools, evidence, artifacts, histories, environments, constraints).
- **Chef**: The dynamic configuration or operator process that selects and binds an appropriate operational constellation for an encounter.
- **Beast**: The temporary, embodied operational configuration capable of acting. A Beast dissolves when the encounter concludes; its scratchpad memory is ephemeral, but its transaction receipts, scars, and notebook entries commit permanently to the immutable event ledger.

## 2. The Core Techniques

### Technique I — Summon
> **Summon changes what becomes consequentially co-present.**
- Brings previously separated people, capabilities, artifacts, histories, projects, tools, evidence, representations, and environments into mutual visibility.
- **Summon Scar (I)**: $\text{Observed relation} \neq \text{Proven lineage}$.
- Epistemic Partitioning:
  - `OBSERVED`: Physically verified element or direct empirical receipt.
  - `INFERRED`: Derived hypothesis; unverified candidate.
  - `CANDIDATE_RELATION`: Co-present correlation without historical derivation.
  - `UNKNOWN`: Explicit representation of missing presence (never hallucinated).

### Alchemy — Transmute
> **Transmute uses consequential co-presence to create a grounded new form that the field does not currently express.**
- Derives new structural expressions (briefs, concepts, treatments, code architectures, platform packaging) from co-present source material.
- **Transmute Scar (II)**: $\text{Generated possibility} \neq \text{Established reality}$.
- Status Partitioning:
  - `SOURCE_FACT`: Verifiable ground truth directly present in source inputs.
  - `SUPPORTED_DERIVATION`: Structurally linked inference with intact dependency provenance.
  - `CREATIVE_PROPOSAL`: Generative hypothesis awaiting stakeholder evaluation.
  - `REQUIRES_VALIDATION`: Blocker requiring empirical or authority check.
  - `AUTHORIZED`: Formally signed off by sovereign key or designated decision owner.
  - `EXECUTED`: Physically committed through engine transaction.

### Joy Boy Reground
> **Release accumulated path, framing, and representation privilege while preserving objective, evidence, scars, demonstrated capability, consequential distinctions, authority, and unresolved residue; reconstruct from the capability available now.**
- Purges obsolete path bias, conversational scaffolding, and representation privilege.
- Carries forward: Immutable SQLite transaction digests, verified scars, active qualified capabilities, explicit deficits, and unresolved residue.

## 3. The 10 Qualification Criteria for Candidate Techniques

Every candidate technique must declare and physically demonstrate:
1. **Object**: What does it transform?
2. **Input State**: What must be present?
3. **Operation**: What materially changes?
4. **Output State**: What exists afterward that did not exist before?
5. **Conservation**: What must remain invariant?
6. **Authority**: What may the technique change, and what remains human-controlled?
7. **Failure Signature**: How does the technique characteristically go wrong?
8. **Null Case**: When should it do nothing?
9. **Evidence**: What demonstrates that it contributed capability beyond strong ordinary reasoning?
10. **Carrier**: Where does the capability physically live? (Code, hook, test, skill, profile, or native reasoning).

*Qualification Invariant*: Candidate techniques become qualified ONLY after surviving trials across at least 2 distinct disposition classes with 0 errors.
