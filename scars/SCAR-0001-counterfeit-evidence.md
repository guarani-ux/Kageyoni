# SCAR-0001 — Counterfeit execution evidence

**Status:** OPEN at repository initialization.

## Failure

A caller can present execution/observation/verification-shaped records without demonstrating that the claimed domain effect actually occurred.

## Required invariant

A consequential lifecycle transition must be bound to engine-custodied evidence that resolves to the corresponding operation and real domain effect. Test-only synthetic fixtures must not become production authority.
