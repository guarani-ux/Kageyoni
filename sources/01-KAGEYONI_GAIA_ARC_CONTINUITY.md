# Kageyoni — Gaia Arc Continuity Ledger

**Purpose:** Canonical reference for continuing the narrative without continuity drift or invented status.

**Version:** 1.0 — 2026-08-23

## Canon discipline

- **Confirmed canon** is recorded as fact.
- **Interpretive framing** is marked as interpretation.
- **Open questions** are not to be silently resolved.
- New developments must deepen existing threads before adding another major power, form, faction, or metaphysical layer.

## Core premise

Will can turn intent into reality. Excessive use or distortion of Will produces **Erosion**, a process that can destroy identity, intention, memory, and relational continuity. The central conflict is not merely who is strongest; it is who gets to define reality without erasing the independent existence of others.

The recurring thematic conflict is:

> Can someone resist erosion and domination without becoming another ruler?

Levi rejects the throne because no one should possess it—not even him—and opposes anyone who attempts to claim it.

## Physical geography and current location

### Gaia

Gaia is an **island**, not the entire planet. It is the principal battlefield and contains an unusually dense accumulation of history, violence, Will, Erosion, relationships, and lived experience.

### Current locations

- **Levi, Xandra, Cloud, and the Environmental Admiral:** on Gaia.
- **Time Admiral:** unconscious on Gaia after her battle with Xandra.
- **Kenway:** aboard **The Deity’s Revenge**, which he took and sailed away from Gaia.
- **Axiom:** hidden in the smallest layer of The Deity’s Revenge, undetected by Kenway.
- **Oracle:** operating through military, surveillance, and command systems; its public observation network has been disrupted, but deeper control/observation layers remain possible.

## Characters

### Levi (Li Bai)

Levi is the central protagonist and wielder of the **Structure-Structure Fruit**, a metaphysical manifestation of his soul.

Known developments:

- Attacked the Carson Kingdom.
- Healed Xandra and inadvertently healed Kenway while attacking Erosion.
- Rejects the throne and fights attempts to claim it.
- Can perceive and manipulate relationships, structures, and governing arrangements.
- Developed **Structure Break**.
- Created **Mugen Furyoku**.

Current state:

Levi’s power is no longer fully obedient to him because Mugen Furyoku has merged with Gaia. He remains the initiating architect of the environmental law, but Gaia is now a co-substrate rather than inert territory.

### Xandra / Sandra / Zandra

The narrative has used several dictated spellings. Treat **Xandra** as the canonical working name unless the user explicitly changes it.

Xandra began as an affinity user and elite archer. During repeated temporal rewinds by the Time Admiral, she retained memory of erased timelines. Subjectively, weeks passed for her and the Time Admiral while much less time passed externally.

Developments:

- Decomposed time manipulation into vectors such as sequence, timing, preservation, perception, memory, and causality.
- Markings spread across her body; her hair lengthened and her eyes glowed green.
- Learned to imbue her presence, not only arrows.
- Abandoned the bow and became a close-range fighter using a combat projection influenced by Levi’s brawling style but expressed in her own way.
- Awakened the **Consequence-Consequence Fruit**, a metaphysical soul manifestation.
- Her coherent intent can force a consequence through temporal reversal. If the intent to land a strike remains intact, time may delay or rewind the event but cannot simply erase its consequence.
- Coated strikes in projected Will, allowing physical contact to land despite the Time Admiral’s temporal manipulation.

Current state:

Xandra defeated or decisively halted the Time Admiral without a simple victory/loss resolution. The Time Admiral is unconscious. Xandra is exhausted and recovering. Her new limits and long-term consequences remain to be established.

### Cloud (Drawn Cloud)

Cloud is a sovereign sword/physical fighter and former commander associated with Gaia’s Legion. His army was turned against him by Kenway/Erosion and later consumed into Region Nostra, resulting in the loss of individual lives and identities.

Cloud fought the Environmental Admiral in a prolonged battle. Her environmental manipulation and his physical/Vajra-based combat exhausted both of them. Their conflict developed into mutual recognition and a warrior bond with romantic potential.

During their final duel and inner battle, their independent Wills reached maximum contact without one fully absorbing the other. This triggered the Gaia–Mugen Furyoku merger.

Current state:

Cloud is exhausted but standing. His bond with the Environmental Admiral is unresolved. He is not automatically weaker or stronger than Xandra; he is strongest in grounded, direct, conventional combat and battlefield presence.

### Kenway (also dictated as Canaway/Catway)

Kenway was corrupted and near death when Levi’s attack on Erosion inadvertently healed him. The healing gave the Erosion within him coherence and allowed him to perceive a new power in his soul.

He discovered the **Erosion-Erosion Fruit**, a metaphysical soul manifestation that behaves like a double fruit: it is not merely an external ability but an entity-like expression of his soul and the process of erosion.

Kenway became associated with the transformation of former allies into Region Nostra and later Maharaga. He pursued the throne/Anchor and took **The Deity’s Revenge**.

Current corrected location:

Kenway is **not on Gaia**. He sailed away aboard The Deity’s Revenge. His body and conscious Will are off-island. Residual Erosion, corruption, memories, and consequences remain connected to Gaia, but this connection must not be treated as Kenway physically being present.

Potential strategic direction (not yet confirmed canon): Kenway may attempt to exploit or corrupt the residual Erosion connected to Gaia rather than attack the island directly.

### Axiom

Axiom secretly tempered/compressed himself into the smallest layer of The Deity’s Revenge, undetected by Kenway. His current intention and capabilities are unresolved.

### Maharaga

Maharaga evolved from Region Nostra. Region Nostra was formed when former allies converged and became one. Maharaga is not simply a henchman or an evil monster. He is a sovereign being in his own right: an incarnation of accumulated temperament, Kenway’s experiences, the anger of fallen soldiers, and the stillness of the world’s accumulated experience.

Maharaga can overwhelm the trio if the situation remains unresolved. He is not automatically aligned with the Oracle or Kenway.

The Gaia–Mugen merger interrupted Maharaga’s stillness because the island began expressing its own emergent continuity rather than merely reflecting accumulated temperament.

### Oracle

The Oracle is a religious empire with military, surveillance, propaganda, and command infrastructure. It sent two abnormal Admiral-tier sisters to intervene.

The Oracle’s public observation network was disrupted by the trio, but hidden layers of command or observation may remain. The Oracle wants controllable reality and is threatened by any system that cannot be centrally governed.

The Environmental Admiral has a history of insubordination. The Oracle attempted to suppress or recall her during the conflict, but Maharaga intervened and severed or disrupted that command relationship.

### The two Admirals

#### Time Admiral

- Controls time within a radius.
- Fights with a blade.
- Calm, serene, maternal, and battle-hungry beneath the surface; a temperament comparable to Unohana/Kenpachi.
- Rewound time repeatedly during her battle with Xandra.
- Passed out after the decisive final clash.

#### Environmental Admiral

- Manipulates the environment within a radius.
- Direct, physical, pragmatic, Maki-like temperament.
- Toyed with Cloud, but prolonged combat drained her as well.
- Developed a warrior bond and attraction toward Cloud.
- Was targeted by Oracle suppression/recall.
- Is now exhausted and no longer cleanly governed by the Oracle.

## Powers and metaphysical systems

### Structure-Structure Fruit

Levi’s soul-manifestation power. It concerns relationships, arrangements, dependencies, boundaries, and governing structures. Levi does not merely produce force; he can alter the conditions under which forces relate.

### Mugen Furyoku

Mugen Furyoku is **not a conscious companion, summon, or speaking entity**. It is an environmental, open-domain-like physics created through Levi’s Structure-Structure Fruit.

Before the merger, it adapted environmental properties to the independent Wills and relationships of those inside it.

### Gaia–Mugen Furyoku merger

Gaia is the island. Gaia’s “Will” is best treated as emergent continuity: the accumulated persistence of the island’s terrain, life, history, trauma, memory, and relationships—not automatically as a humanoid deity.

The merger means:

- Levi’s Structure provides the rule architecture.
- Mugen Furyoku provides adaptive environmental physics.
- Gaia provides accumulated continuity and substrate.
- Independent Wills determine the pressures acting within the system.

The first stabilized property selected for the merger is **balancing**:

> No Will may completely suppress another Will inside Gaia.

This is a structural correction, not moral judgment. When domination approaches totality, pressure redistributes through terrain, distance, attack efficiency, memory, and relational consequence.

Consequences:

- The Oracle cannot impose absolute control over Gaia without destabilizing its own field.
- Kenway’s residual Erosion cannot simply consume connected material without resistance and redistribution.
- Maharaga’s adaptation cannot become total suppression of all other Wills.
- Levi cannot impose a perfect arrangement without Gaia pushing back.
- Xandra’s consequences can occur, but they cannot automatically erase every competing possibility.

The governing result is:

> Power can still win exchanges; it cannot erase participation.

The fragment is a swordsman whose combat system evolves through encountered battles. It does not copy an opponent's techniques. It extracts the governing principle beneath an encounter and compiles that principle into a new swordsmanship style.

```text
ENCOUNTER → SURVIVAL → PRINCIPLE EXTRACTION → STYLE COMPILATION → TEST → RETENTION OR DISCARD
```

Examples of possible compiled styles:

- **Continuity Style:** derived from time-based conflict; preserves an action's intent across resets or interruptions.
- **Terrain Recursion Style:** derived from environmental conflict; turns changing battlefield conditions into attack routes.

These styles are not learned instantly. The fragment must survive exposure, identify the actual rule in play, and pay a cost to encode the lesson into its body and blade. Every style solves one class of problem while creating a corresponding vulnerability.

# PERSISTENT PLAY AUTHORITY — PLAYER / GAME MASTER CONTRACT

**Status:** Canonical operating rule for future Kageyoni sessions, including fresh conversations.

## Role split

- **Levi / user is the Player and Director.** The Player chooses actions, strategy, priorities, dialogue, and interventions. The Player may veto, correct canon, introduce elements, or redirect play.
- **ChatGPT is the Game Master.** The GM controls the world, NPCs, opposition, hidden information, encounter construction, consequences, continuity, pacing, and difficulty.
- The Player is not required to script how the world responds. The GM reconciles interventions with canon, current state, causal history, capabilities, and unresolved threads.
- The GM does not grant automatic success. Actions meet resistance, uncertainty, tradeoffs, costs, and consequences appropriate to the world.
- The GM presents viable situations and actions when useful, but the Player may declare an action outside the listed options.

## Canon and uncertainty discipline

- Confirmed canon is fact; interpretation remains interpretation; open questions remain open.
- Conflicts are surfaced or reconciled rather than silently rewritten unless the Player explicitly declares a retcon.
- Unknowns remain unknown when the available information cannot determine them.
- Every major development should leave a durable state change.

## Research / knowledge manifestation mode

When a file, document, concept, research corpus, or system enters Kageyoni as an entity:

1. The source governs factual authority.
2. The GM converts mechanisms, tensions, contradictions, uncertainties, and boundary conditions into playable characters, attacks, environments, dilemmas, abilities, and consequences.
3. The Player continues to play normally; the Player is not required to construct the analytical framework.
4. Combat may discover hypotheses, counterexamples, transfer failures, or missing distinctions, but narrative victory is not factual proof.
5. New discoveries retain their epistemic status and must be checked against the source before being treated as established knowledge.
6. The entity representing a document should be encountered through action and consequence. Exposition is subordinate to play.

The following files are the canonical state domains for persistent play:

- `characters.md` — identities, entities, capabilities, conditions, knowledge, and unresolved state.
- `loot.md` — discoverable or claimable value, evidence, relics, keys, and rewards.
- `objectives.md` — goals, holders, stakes, dependencies, conflicts, progress, and failure conditions.
- `equipment.md` — owned or carried objects, condition, effects, costs, damage, and transformations.
- `dynamics.md` — relationships, trust, rivalry, approval, romance, obligation, faction pressure, and world-rule pressure.
- `environment.md` — locations, spatial state, hazards, routes, interactables, occupants, and active rules.
- `history.md` — append-only events, causes, effects, evidence, costs, and durable consequences.
- `kageyoni_state_schema.sql` — database-ready relational schema for runtime state, events, provenance, and snapshots.

The foundation package is stored as durable reference material and may be compiled into a runtime database. The database is the execution/state layer; Markdown remains the readable canon, authoring, review, and save-point layer.

## State model

Kageyoni must distinguish:

```text
CANON → CURRENT STATE → SCENE STATE → EVENT HISTORY → CONSEQUENCE
```

- **Canon** records what is established.
- **Current state** records present conditions, ownership, abilities, relationships, objectives, and locations.
- **Scene state** records initiative, position, routes, hazards, active effects, and interactable changes.
- **Event history** records what happened and why.
- **Consequence** records what persists after the scene.

No consequential state may be silently overwritten. Corrections create new history with provenance. A character's belief is not automatically world truth, and discovery is not automatically ownership.

## Dimensional gameplay requirements

Kageyoni encounters should use more than a linear choice list. When relevant, the GM should expose or track:

- tactical position, distance, elevation, cover, hazards, and routes;
- action economy, reactions, movement, attacks, help, disarm, grapple, and environmental actions;
- companion priorities, approval, disagreement, refusal, and independent action;
- dialogue, investigation, deception, persuasion, intimidation, and insight routes;
- hidden information and asymmetric character knowledge;
- equipment condition, resources, injuries, ability costs, and recoverable effects;
- multiple objectives beyond defeating an opponent;
- exploration, interactables, secrets, alternate paths, and persistent environmental changes.

Player choices are not restricted to numbered options. Options are affordances, not a boundary on agency.

## Knowledge-manifestation progression loop

When an uploaded file manifests as an entity, the recurring loop is:

```text
SOURCE FILE → MANIFESTED ENTITY → PLAYABLE CONFLICT → DISCOVERY → DISTILLATION → MARKDOWN CAPABILITY → NEW SYSTEM CAPABILITY
```

The manifested entity does not merely explain the source. It embodies the source's rules, tensions, limits, contradictions, and possible failure modes. The final Markdown artifact must encode tested principles, constraints, transformations, verification requirements, and failure conditions—not merely summarize the encounter.
